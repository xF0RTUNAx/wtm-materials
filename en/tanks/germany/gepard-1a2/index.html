Gepard 1A2

Rank X Germany Researchable

Ground campaign · updated 18 Jan 2026

MobilityArmourArmamentFeaturesVerdictRatingTree

Mobility

Pros: good mobility thanks to the Leopard 1 chassis - a high top speed and decent agility on roads and firm ground; lets you take up positions and change firing positions quickly.

Cons: the large size and weight (it is a tank chassis) make manoeuvring in tight urban areas difficult; the running gear and suspension are not built for extreme off-road speeds.

Armour and survivability

Pros: the sturdy Leopard 1 chassis gives basic protection and mobility. The crew and component compartments are designed to stay in the fight.

Cons: the protection is almost entirely "light" - the armour only stops shrapnel and small arms; it is vulnerable to ATGMs, tank guns and aircraft. The tall turret and open fighting compartment make the vehicle easy to spot and vulnerable when hit.

Armament

Main armament: 2 x 35 mm autocannons (Oerlikon KDA variants) in a twin-gun turret, stabilised and fitted with a partly automated aiming system.

Shells:

It uses various 35×228 mm rounds (for example HE, HE-frag and armour-piercing incendiary / fragmentation incendiary types), and the high combined rate of fire of both guns puts up a dense wall of fire against aircraft and light armour.

Features:

Radar detection and target tracking let it spot low-flying targets and lay the guns on them effectively; 360° turret traverse and high gun elevation.

Features

The Stinger missiles with radar-assisted targeting are the biggest difference from its predecessor. It performs excellently in defence and as air cover; it is weak against direct attacks by armour and anti-tank weapons and needs cover.

Verdict

In game, the Gepard 1A2 is a short-range air defence specialist: its high rate of fire, radar and ability to react quickly to air threats make it indispensable for protecting your team. At the same time, it is vulnerable in duels with armour and against ATGMs, so it needs smart positioning, support from allies and good use of nearby cover. On top of that, it has missiles that are very effective against aircraft. And thanks to the APDS belt with 120mm of penetration, it is effective against medium vehicles even at high ranks. The best missile SPAA at the time of writing.
